The premise

Most simulations predict what will happen. We are building one that asks a different question — what could happen, if the conditions of human life were a little bit different?

— A possibility engine, not a forecast

What we build

Three modes of simulation.

Each world is a self-contained experiment. Simple rules at the bottom. Surprising behavior at the top.

The architecture

Agents that behave, not perform.

Most generative agents pretend to be people. Ours are built from the ground up to behave like them — with the same layers of biology, feeling, memory, and culture that shape real human action.

L0

Substrate — the body

Inherited traits that shape what an agent can do. Vision, metabolism, the slow drift of capacities across generations.

L1

Affect — the feeling

Always running underneath. Emotions are not decoration; they color every choice, and they remember.

L2

Deliberation — the choice

What the agent considers when there is time to consider. Slower, more uncertain, more recognizably ours.

L3

The social — the others

No agent is alone. Behavior moves through relationships, organizations, and unspoken agreements.

L4

Meaning — the story

Beliefs about how the world works. Myths, ideologies, the structures that make some actions feel right and others unthinkable.

L5

Awareness — the witness

The capacity to notice oneself. Most agents in most simulations don't have this. Ours can.

Foundations

Standing on the right shoulders.

Generative social science is a sixty-year tradition. We build on it carefully, with sources from anthropology, neuroscience, complex systems, and the philosophy of play.

α

Generative social science

If you didn't grow it, you didn't explain it. Agent-based modeling as method, from Schelling onward.

β

Embodied cognition

Mind is not separable from feeling. Decisions live in the body before they reach the language of choice.

γ

Cultural evolution

Behavior is inherited by more channels than DNA. What gets passed down — and how — shapes everything.

δ

Complex adaptive systems

Order without a planner. Hidden patterns underneath what looks like chaos.

ε

Finite and infinite play

Some societies play to win. Others play to continue the play. The difference shows up everywhere.

ζ

Archetypal psychology

Characters are not data structures. They have soul codes. The persona is the door, not the prison.
